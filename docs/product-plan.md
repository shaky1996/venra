# Product Plan

## Product summary

Venra is a carrier readiness platform for trucking companies. It is intended to give carrier teams a clear view of whether their drivers, equipment, documents, and compliance obligations are ready for operation.

Instead of spreading critical information across folders, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems, Venra will gradually bring it into one clean workspace. The long-term product may cover drivers, trucks, trailers, documents, expirations, inspections, tickets, claims, insurance recovery, IFTA records, company compliance, and FMCSA data.

## Product principles

- Show users what needs attention without overcrowding the interface.
- Build features in small, useful phases rather than all at once.
- Keep records easy to find, understand, and maintain.
- Treat AI as a future assistant for retrieval and guidance, not as the initial product foundation.
- Favor dependable workflows and clear data over unnecessary complexity.

## Initial product phases

1. Establish the repository, documentation, and development conventions.
2. Create the frontend foundation and reusable visual theme.
3. Create the backend foundation and local PostgreSQL environment.
4. Deliver one small, end-to-end carrier workflow.
5. Expand into additional readiness and compliance modules based on validated needs.

Detailed feature requirements and release priorities will be added as each phase begins.
